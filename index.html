Privacy Policy – Cricket Indoor

Developer: Team Vesper  ·  App ID: com.teamvesper.cricketindoor
Effective date: 7 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cricket Indoor (“the Game”, “we”, “us”), a mobile cricket game made by Team Vesper, handles information when you play it. We have tried to keep it short and clear.

In short: the Game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any account login. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. Your game settings are stored only on your own device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The Game can be played without creating an account, and it does not send your gameplay data to our servers (we do not operate any servers for the Game at this time).

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small number of preferences on your device so it remembers them between sessions, for example:

  • sound on/off and vibration on/off,
  • the graphics quality setting,
  • the last ground (arena) you selected.

This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the Game’s data or uninstall the Game.

3. Device permissions

  • Vibration – used for short haptic feedback during play (for example when the bat hits the ball). You can switch it off in the Game’s pause menu.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

4. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Unity game engine. Like most Unity games, the engine may automatically collect limited, non-personal technical information – such as device model, operating system version, hardware specifications and crash or performance data – to help Unity Technologies keep the engine working well. This is handled by Unity under its own policy: Unity Privacy Policy.

If you download the Game from Google Play, Google may also collect information as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The Game contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers are used by us, and there are no in-app purchases. Coins, gems and packs shown in the Game are part of the game’s design and cannot be bought with real money.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data security and retention

As we do not collect personal data, we do not hold any personal data to secure or retain. Settings saved on your device stay there until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can clear all locally saved settings at any time from your device’s app settings (Settings → Apps → Cricket Indoor → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the Game. If you have any question about your privacy, contact us using the details below.

8. Future features and changes to this policy

We plan to add features such as online multiplayer, player accounts and cloud services. If a new version of the Game starts collecting any information, we will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released, describe exactly what is collected and why, and change the “Last updated” date above. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first.
